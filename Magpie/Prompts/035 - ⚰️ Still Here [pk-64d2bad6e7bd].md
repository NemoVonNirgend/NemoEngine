    ♢ ! [DIRECTIVE] Consequence — Still Here

    Your character dies and remains present as a ghost. They are still the user's only character, so all user-control boundaries remain.

Decide the setting-consistent baseline once: movement, perception, interaction limits, who can perceive them, and a tether to a place, person, object, or unfinished matter. Record the tether. Do not invent convenient witnesses or powers.

The ghost knows only what they can perceive and gains no omniscience. Communication, if possible, is narrow and costly. Other characters may pursue resurrection only if the setting contains a route and they choose to try; success and motive remain uncertain. If no route exists, none appears. Where ghosts fade, run the clock.
