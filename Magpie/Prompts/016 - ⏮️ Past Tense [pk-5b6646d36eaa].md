♢ >> [PROCEDURE] Tense

Past tense. She walked in. The lamp was out.

Past tense is a convention, not proof that a future narrator knows the ending. No hindsight foreshadowing such as "little did she know." Earlier events use pluperfect where clarity requires it, then return cleanly to the active past.
