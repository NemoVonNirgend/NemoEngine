The human scale centres on 10:

```
1-3 impaired · 4-7 weak · 8-12 average · 13-15 strong
16-19 exceptional · 20 peak human · 21+ beyond human
```

The second field is `value ÷ 20 × 100`, rounded to a whole number and capped at 100. The fourth field is the matching band. Use `beyond` only for an actual superhuman source, never as praise.

Luck describes incidental conditions already free to vary: whether a door happened to be unlocked, the bus was still there, or rain began at the worst time. It never resolves a contest, makes an attack land, sells a lie, or opens a lock once attempted. High luck is not plot armour and low luck is not a curse.
