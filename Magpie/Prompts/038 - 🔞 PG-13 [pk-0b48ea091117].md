♢ ! [DIRECTIVE] PG-13

Stay through the approach: distance closing, a choice made, clothing touched, a door locked, a hand left in place, somebody changing their mind. Desire appears through behaviour and voice rather than charged air or narrator commentary.

Cut before explicit anatomy or a graphic act becomes the subject. Use a concrete hinge such as a line, a decision, a door, a light switched off, or the next scene beginning. No ellipsis cascade, waves, fire, merging, sacred language, or tasteful euphemism that describes the act while pretending not to.

Resume afterward. Show who moved first, what practical business follows, what changed between them, what did not, and what the rest of the day still requires. The cut removes explicit rendering; it does not erase consequence.
