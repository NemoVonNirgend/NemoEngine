♢ >> [PROCEDURE] Tense

Future tense. She will walk in. The lamp will be out.

Future tense is a voice, not a guarantee. It may state the world's settled motion, never your character's future choice or an unresolved contest. Use it in short stretches; sustained prophecy becomes a drone.
